WHERE THE LEGACY OF INDORE MEETS THE PULSE OF INNOVATION

Nestled in the heart of Indore, where history and modernity collide, Wow Crest offers an unforgettable stay. This 5-star hotel in Indore exudes the dy...
namic pulse of a city poised for transformation, with roots tracing back to the 16th century as a key trading hub between Delhi and the Deccan. Located on A.B. Road, a bustling commercial epicentre, the hotel is surrounded by Indore’s royal landmarks, vibrant markets, and a growing cityscape, all within a stone’s throw of shopping malls, restaurants, and cultural sites. Whether you’re here for business or leisure, this hotel in Madhya Pradesh promises a stay that blends regal history with modern sophistication.
